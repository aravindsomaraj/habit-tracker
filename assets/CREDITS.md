# Visual asset credits

- `images/google-signin-dark.svg`: unmodified official Google **Sign in with Google** dark rectangular web button from [Google's downloadable branding assets](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip), member `Android + Web/SVG/Dark/Theme=Dark, Show text=Yes, Shape=Square, Platform=Android+Web.svg`. Google branding is subject to [Google's branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), not the project's artwork license. Text is outlined; no external font or Google script is loaded.

- `fonts/VT323-Regular.ttf`: VT323 by Peter Hull, downloaded from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/vt323. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full notice is retained in `fonts/OFL-VT323.txt`. Served locally, with a monospace fallback.
- `images/woodland.svg`: original pixel landscape created for this repository. No Minecraft artwork, logos, characters, or textures are used. Available under the project's license.
- The journal-spine brand mark, pixel navigation icons, and button treatments are original CSS/SVG artwork. They require no external assets. The landscape uses a muted dusk palette of stone, charcoal, and copper.
- Existing `images/cat-1.png`, `cat-2.png`, and `cat-3.png` are pre-existing repository assets, retained for the habit mood feature; their original provenance is not established by this redesign.

Motion is limited to existing completion confetti and brief control/toast feedback. The retro stylesheet disables decorative motion and transitions when reduced motion is requested.
